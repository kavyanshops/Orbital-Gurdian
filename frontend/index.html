📖 Technical Explanation

Why SGP4?

SGP4 is the standard propagator for TLE data. It includes perturbations for Earth's oblateness (J2), atmospheric drag, and solar/lunar effects.

Why ECI?

ECI (Earth-Centered Inertial) avoids Earth rotation complexity when computing distances between objects.
